HOW TO READ THE RESULTS
  Expected   = what a team would estimate before seeing the AI answer
  Result     = SprintIQ's suggested points and its likely range (70% interval)
  Confidence = how precise the estimate is (narrow range = high, wide range = low)
  Verdict    PASS        matches the expected size or behaviour
             PARTIAL     the expected value is inside the range, the main number is higher
             LIMITATION  a known weakness of the model (explained at the end)

    < huge (20) < programme (40) < mega (100).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHY IS CONFIDENCE ONLY 25% FOR THE BILLING EPIC (20 POINTS)?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Confidence here means precision, not "chance of being right":

     confidence = 1 - (width of the range / the estimate) / 1.5

 For "Migrate the billing platform to a multi-region datastore":
   - It has 18 components; the training data stops at 7. So the model cannot measure
     it and extrapolates from two directions:
        trend continued                     -> about 15 points
        biggest known story, scaled up      -> about 28 points
   - The final estimate is between them: 20.8, with a raw range of 12.9 to 36.2.
   - That range (23.3 points wide) is wider than the estimate itself:
        1 - (23.3 / 20.8) / 1.5 = 0.25  ->  25%
   - On top of that, anything beyond the training data is capped at 30% at most.

 For the panel: low confidence is correct here. The system is saying "about 20, could
 be 13 or could be 40, split it", which is the honest answer for a story bigger than
 anything it has seen. Normal stories (TC-04 to TC-08) get 51-63%.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 LIMITATIONS FOUND BY THESE TESTS (and what would fix them)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Small items are estimated a bit high (TC-01, TC-02): the lowest answer is 2-3.
    Cause: the training dataset has very few 1-point stories.
    Fix:   retrain with SprintIQ's own recorded estimates, which include small work.

 2. FIXED (model 2.3.0): small work typed as "Story" was over-estimated (TC-03: 8).
    Cause: every SprintIQ "Story" was mapped to the dataset's "feature" type, which
           averages 5-13 points.
    Fix:   a Story with 3 components or fewer, no external dependency, no security work
           and 40 words or fewer is now read as an "enhancement" (a change to something
           existing). Bigger Stories stay features, so large cases did not move.
           Result: TC-03 8 -> 5, TC-12 5 -> 3, all large cases unchanged.
    Still open: TC-04 / TC-05 (5-6 components) stay at 8, and small items cannot go
           below the floor in limitation 1.

 3. FIXED (model 2.3.0): the words "export" / "import" in a story's text no longer count
    as integration work (a person's "export" or "import" label still does).
    Result: TC-13 8 -> 5.
