{{docAuthor}}Nadeesha Kumari·Updated 2 hours ago·6 min read

Summary. This spec defines OAuth2 SSO for the payroll app across Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID, including token handling, session persistence, and audit requirements.

Overview

Enterprise customers require single sign-on before rolling the payroll app out to their organizations. We support the OAuth2 authorization-code flow with PKCE, linking existing email accounts to a chosen identity provider on first sign-in.
